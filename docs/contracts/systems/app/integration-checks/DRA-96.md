## Rules

Integration checks for DRA-96, on top of the required browser checks and every earlier check above, as reworded below (all must still pass). Each check starts in a fresh page unless it says otherwise. Background: the committed `game/tuning/debug_spawns.json` keeps its other rows, its `play`, `mode` and `loop_ticks`, and gains these three planet rows, in this order (scope report DRA-96, Limits: one planet, and two with overlapping fields):
1. `{"type": "planet", "x": 320}` (enters from the top, straight over the respawn point)
2. `{"type": "planet", "x": 200}` (enters from the top)
3. `{"type": "planet", "x": 440, "y": 289}` (placed so it appears level with planet 2, their fields overlapping)

Checks marked **(planet mode)** need `"play": "list"`, `"mode": "planet"` and `"loop_ticks": 1200` in that file: the Director switches them, or allows the Integration Engineer to switch them for the run and put them back after. In that mode the three rows are placed on ticks 1, 401 and 801 (ids 1 to 3) and again every 1200 ticks. A planet shows its entry warning for 60 ticks where it will appear, then appears and moves only with the world, 1.1 px a tick down, never sideways. Row 3 is placed 400 ticks after row 2, so it appears 440 px lower, exactly where planet 2 has scrolled to. The planet colors: body (140, 70, 200), rings (180, 120, 255). Ticks are the run's own; times are ticks ÷ 60. Positions are on the 640 × 480 canvas.
