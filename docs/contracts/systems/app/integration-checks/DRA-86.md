In that mode the eight rows are placed on ticks 1, 151, 301, 451, 601, 751, 901 and 1051 (ids 1 to 8), 165 px of scroll apart, so no two islands overlap. An island has no warning: it is on the field from the tick it is placed, and moves only with the world: still until tick 31, then 1.1 px a tick down, never sideways. The island colors: fill (70, 160, 80), edge (35, 90, 45). Ticks are the run's own; times are ticks ÷ 60. Positions are on the 640 × 480 canvas. "F3 off" means the overlay hidden, so its outlines don't cover the island's outer pixels.
