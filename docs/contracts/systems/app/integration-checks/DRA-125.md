# App, integration checks, DRA-125
Part of: app
Read when: you run or extend the browser checks for the screens: menu, instructions, leaderboard, pause, last life and game over (DRA-125).

## Rules

Integration checks for DRA-125, on top of the required browser checks and every earlier check, as reworded below (all must still pass). Each check starts in a fresh page unless it says otherwise. Background:
- `game/tuning/last_life.json` exists and holds `last_life_delay` from 02-game-flow.md, Values, and the burst stand-ins from scope report DRA-125, Values needed (Burst), each key as `docs/contracts/schemas/last-life.schema.json` names it.
- Ticks are the run's own; times are ticks ÷ 60. Positions are on the 640 × 480 canvas. The stand-in screens are white default-font text on the black background; "matches reference text" means text rendered with pygame's default font at the size the contract names, white, antialiased, compared by its ink. The sizes, texts, boxes and burst numbers below are the scope report's stand-ins (DRA-125, Values needed), quoted so the checks can be read on the canvas; if the committed files hold other values, read the check with those.
- To reach play: after the click, press Enter on the menu (Play is selected). "Play start" below and in every earlier check is that press.

Earlier checks, reworded for DRA-125:
- **Every check that starts at play start** (DRA-6 to DRA-117): the first frames after the click show the menu, not the run. Press Enter once (or Space), release it, and the check runs from there as written; its ticks and times count from that press. A check that holds an arrow "from play start" presses the arrow after Enter.
- **Every game over** (DRA-23, DRA-61, DRA-62, DRA-76, DRA-117 and the drifter checks that end a run): after the last `life lost lives=0`, the log shows INFO `last life score=<n>`, not `game over meters=<n>`. About `last_life_delay` ÷ 60 s later (2 s with the GDD's value) it shows INFO `game over score=<n>` with the same number, then INFO `screen game_over`. The box is the game-over screen below, not DRA-23's: no "Game Over" in mixed case, no "Press any arrow to retry". The ship never shows under it, after a hit or a fall.
- **Every retry** (DRA-23, DRA-61, DRA-62, DRA-76, DRA-117): a fresh Enter (or Space) on the game-over screen with RETRY selected, not an arrow. Arrows on the game-over screen never start a run. "Everything freezes" is read on the game-over screen, after the last-life delay, not on the tick the last life is lost.
- **DRA-23, Retry needs a fresh press**: now with Enter. Hold Enter from before the game-over screen shows (during the last life) and keep holding it 2 s after: no `new run`. Release, press: INFO `new run`, then `screen playing`.
- **Every HUD check** (DRA-13, DRA-23, DRA-61, DRA-117): read on the run's frames, after play start. The menu, instructions and leaderboard frames have no HUD.
- **Tuning loaded** (DRA-111, DRA-117 and earlier): the `scoring loaded ...` line is followed by `last life loaded last_life_delay=<n> burst_particle_count=<n> burst_particle_speed=<x> burst_particle_size=<n>` with the values the committed `last_life.json` holds (the GDD's delay and the scope report's burst stand-ins). Source: tuning-loader part (Last life file).

New checks:
- **The menu (`/` and `?debug=1`):** on the first frame after the click, white text matching `DRIFTWARD ACE` (32 px) above `Play`, `Instructions` and `Leaderboard` (24 px) in that order, each centered across the canvas, the items' tops 40 px apart, a `>` (24 px) left of `Play` only. No starfield, no HUD, no hearts. `screen ...` is not logged until a screen changes. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Presentation (Menu); 02-game-flow.md, rule 2; scope report DRA-125, Values needed.
- **Menu wraps (`?debug=1`):** press ArrowUp: the `>` is left of `Leaderboard`, and `menu select leaderboard` is logged. ArrowDown: back on `Play` (`menu select play`). ArrowDown twice: `Leaderboard`; ArrowDown again: `Play`. Holding ArrowDown for 1 s moves it once. ArrowLeft and ArrowRight do nothing. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Rules (MENU, Presses); 02-game-flow.md, menu_navigation.
- **Instructions (`?debug=1`):** select Instructions, press Enter: `screen instructions`; the canvas shows `HOW TO PLAY` (32 px) and six sections in this order, each a 16 × 16 white outline square left of its title, then its line (16 px): FLY, PAUSE, GRAZE, WARNING, FALL, COINS, with the scope report's lines word for word; and `Press Enter or Space to go back.` (16 px) near the bottom. Every line is centered across the canvas and inside it. Press Space: `screen menu`, the menu shows with `>` on `Play`. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Presentation (Instructions page) and Rules (MENU); 10-ui.md, instructions_page; scope report DRA-125, Values needed.
- **Leaderboard (`/`):** select Leaderboard, press Enter: `LEADERBOARD` (32 px), `No scores yet.` (16 px) and the footer, no HUD. Press Enter: the menu, `>` on `Play`. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Presentation (Leaderboard page); 02-game-flow.md, rule 4.
- **One press, one action (`?debug=1`):** on the menu, hold Enter for 1 s while Play is selected: `screen playing` once, and no `screen paused` while it is held. Release and press Enter again: `pause by=key`, `screen paused`. On the instructions page, hold Space for 1 s: `screen menu` once, and the menu stays on Play: no `screen playing`. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Rules (Presses); 02-game-flow.md, rule 12.
- **Keys held through the click (`?debug=1`):** hold Space while clicking the canvas to start and for 1 s after: the menu stays, nothing is picked. Release and press Space: `screen playing`. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Rules (Start).
- **Pause by key (`?debug=1`, debug list or waves):** during play, press Space: `screen paused`; a box 320 × 120 at (160, 180) where the stars are darker, with `PAUSE` (32 px) centered in it; the HUD and the hearts still show. Two canvas reads 2 s apart are identical; the overlay's numbers don't change; no `drifter`, `scroll`, `wave` or `respawn` line appears. ArrowUp and ArrowLeft during the pause change nothing on the canvas. Press Enter: `screen playing`, and the world moves on from where it stopped, the ship with the speed it had. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Rules (PLAYING, PAUSED); 02-game-flow.md, rule 7; scope report DRA-125, Values needed (Pause box).
- **Pause by focus (`?debug=1`):** during play, take the focus from the page: switch to another tab and back after 3 s (or, if the browser tools can't switch tabs, blur the window with `browser_evaluate`), then click outside the canvas in a second try. Each time: `focus lost event=<name>`, `pause by=focus`, `screen paused`, the PAUSE box, and the run is where it was before the switch: no life lost, no meters added. Coming back does not resume: still paused after 2 s. Enter resumes. Report which pygame event each action delivered. Source: frame-loop part (Focus); 02-game-flow.md, rule 7 and pause_on_focus_loss; 91-decision-log.md, row 81.
- **Focus loss off the run (`?debug=1`):** take the focus away on the menu, on the instructions page and on the game-over screen: no `screen paused` line, the screen doesn't change. Source: 02-game-flow.md, Edge cases.
- **Pause in the respawn wait (`?debug=1`, no key held):** let the ship fall; while it waits at (320, 240), press Space (paused), hold ArrowUp, press Enter (resumed) still holding ArrowUp: no `respawn wait end`, and the ship stays at `x 320.00 y 240.00`. Release ArrowUp and tap it: `respawn wait end` and the ship rises. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Rules (PAUSED); systems/run.md, `hold_arrows`; 02-game-flow.md, Edge cases.
- **Last life by a fall (`?debug=1`, no key held):** end three waits with a short tap of ArrowUp and let the ship fall each time. On the third fall: `life lost lives=0`, then `last life score=<n>`, no `respawn from`. From that frame: no heart shows; no ship pixel; white squares appear on the bottom rows around the x the ship fell at and move up and out, fading, and stay where they are while the stars move down (they don't follow the scroll). The stars and the drifters keep moving and SCORE stays at `<n>`. Space and focus loss do nothing (no `screen paused`). About 2 s later: `game over score=<n>`, `screen game_over`, and no burst pixel is left. Source: systems/run.md, Rules (The last life, A last-life tick, Burst) and Presentation (Burst); 02-game-flow.md, rule 8; 05-lives-and-damage.md, rule 5.
- **Last life by a hit (`?debug=1`, list mode with hazards):** lose the last life to a hazard: the burst starts at the ship's center, 12 squares flying out evenly; nothing hits or grazes after it (no `drifter touch`, no `drifter graze`); new drifters' `drifter spawn` lines may still come during the 2 s. Source: same; 09-waves.md, rules 2 and 9.
- **The game-over screen (`/` and `?debug=1`):** a box 320 × 160 centered at (160, 160) where the stars are darker, with `GAME OVER` (32 px), the score line `SCORE <n>` (16 px) equal to the HUD's SCORE, `RETRY` and `MENU` (16 px) under it, all centered, and `>` (16 px) left of `RETRY` only. Everything is frozen: two reads 2 s apart are identical. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Presentation (Game-over screen); 02-game-flow.md, rule 10; 10-ui.md, game_over_screen.
- **Game-over selection (`?debug=1`):** ArrowDown: `game over select menu`, `>` left of `MENU`; ArrowDown: `RETRY`; ArrowUp: `MENU`. Holding an arrow from the last life into the screen moves nothing until it is released and pressed again. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Rules (GAME_OVER, Presses).
- **RETRY (`?debug=1`):** with RETRY selected, press Enter: `new run`, the waves' `wave start` (when waves play), `screen playing`; the next frame is a fresh run: SCORE 0, GRAZE 0, three hearts, the ship at the center, no box. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Rules (GAME_OVER); 02-game-flow.md, rule 11.
- **MENU (`?debug=1`):** with MENU selected, press Enter: `screen menu`, the menu with `>` on `Play`, and no `new run` yet. Press Enter: `new run`, then `screen playing`, and a fresh run as after RETRY. Source: systems/game-flow.md, Rules (MENU, GAME_OVER); 02-game-flow.md, rule 2; 91-decision-log.md, row 90.
- **Space and Enter don't scroll the page:** on every screen, the page's scroll position stays 0 and the canvas stays in place when Space or Enter is pressed.
- **Tuned last life (desktop, headless, `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`, `DRIFTWARD_DEBUG=1`):** no code change. In a scratch copy of `game/tuning/`, set `"last_life_delay": 30` in `last_life.json`. Point `app.tuning.TUNING_DIR` at it and call `run()`: the log shows `last life loaded last_life_delay=30 ...` and no error. Source: tuning-loader part (Last life file).
- **Bad last-life file (same set-up):** one at a time in a scratch copy: `"last_life_delay": 0` (message names `tuning/last_life.json` and `last_life_delay must be an integer >= 1`); `"burst_particle_speed": true`; an unknown field `"burst_flash": 1`; and `last_life.json` deleted (a `FileNotFoundError` naming its path). Each time the log shows ERROR `unexpected error, showing the error screen` with that error, and no `error screen failed` line. Source: tuning-loader part (Last life file); game/CONTEXT.md, Error handling.
- **Normal mode (`/`):** the whole loop by pixels only: menu, Play, pause and resume with Space, three falls, the burst, the game-over screen after about 2 s, RETRY, a fresh run; then game over again, ArrowDown, Enter: the menu on Play. No (0, 255, 0) pixels at any point.
- **Package contents:** as for DRA-117, and `core/game_flow.py`, `core/last_life.py`, `ui/screen_look.py`, `ui/menu.py`, `ui/instructions.py`, `ui/leaderboard.py`, `ui/pause_box.py`, `ui/burst_view.py` and `tuning/last_life.json` must be in `game.apk`.
