Integration checks for DRA-111, on top of the required browser checks and every earlier check, as reworded below (all must still pass). Each check starts in a fresh page unless it says otherwise. Background: the committed `game/tuning/debug_spawns.json` keeps its other rows, its `play`, `mode` and `loop_ticks`, and gains these five electric ball rows, in this order (scope report DRA-111, Decision log: one ball in the middle, two far apart, two whose circles overlap):

Checks marked **(ball mode)** need `"play": "list"`, `"mode": "electric_ball"` and `"loop_ticks": 200` in that file: the Director switches them, or allows the Integration Engineer to switch them for the run and put them back after. In that mode the five rows are placed on ticks 1, 41, 81, 121 and 161 (ids 1 to 5) and again every 200 ticks. Each shows its entry warning for 60 ticks at its x against the top edge, then falls straight down from just above it. Rows 2 to 5 fall at ≈ 2.47 px a tick, row 1 at 1 px a tick. Every ball charges for 120 ticks from the state it appears, then fires for 120. The electric ball colors: ball (170, 80, 255); rays and outline (200, 150, 255) at full color; fill (200, 160, 255) at `fill_opacity`; rim (240, 220, 255). Ticks are the run's own; times are ticks ÷ 60. Positions are on the 640 × 480 canvas.
