# App, integration checks, DRA-158
Part of: app
Read when: you run or extend the browser checks for the game audio, first iteration (DRA-158).

## Rules

Integration checks for DRA-158, on top of the required browser checks and every earlier check (all must still pass). Each check starts in a fresh page unless it says otherwise. A browser test cannot hear: the checks read the log lines of `app.audio` and `app.audio_tuning` and the run's own lines, and the Director listens by hand for what the log can't show. Background:
- `game/tuning/audio.json` exists and holds the GDD's `audio_levels` (12-art-and-audio.md) and an `effect_channels`, each key as `docs/contracts/schemas/audio.schema.json` names it.
- `game/assets/audio/` holds `music_loop.ogg`, `sfx_hit.ogg`, `sfx_graze.ogg` and `sfx_coin.ogg`.
- Debug mode (`?debug=1`) logs DEBUG; the `sound` lines below are DEBUG, so the checks that read them run in debug mode.

Checks:
- **Loaded:** the log shows one INFO `audio loaded ...` line with the file's values, once, before the first frame. No ERROR and no `audio off` line. Source: systems/audio.md, Logging.
- **No sound before the gesture (real browser, by hand):** load the page and wait on the start page without a click or key. No `music start` line and no sound. After one click or key press the loop starts on the menu and `music start` is logged once. Source: 15-tech-constraints.md, rule 3; systems/audio.md, Rules 5 and 6. If the browser stays silent after the gesture, the run stops and reports: a contract change, not a fix.
- **The loop carries on (debug mode):** from the menu through Play, the pause (Enter or Space) and its resume, a lost focus, the last life, game over and RETRY, no second `music start` line ever appears, and no line says the music stopped. By hand: the loop plays without a break through all of it. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 5 and 7; 02-game-flow.md, rule 13.
- **Hit (debug mode):** every `life lost lives=<n>` line is followed by one `sound play cue=hit` line on the same tick, for a touch and for a fall, and for the last life. A protected fall or a touch while invulnerable logs no `sound play cue=hit`. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 2; systems/run.md, Rules, Sound cues.
- **Graze (debug mode):** every `drifter graze id=<n> type=<t>` line is followed by one `sound play cue=graze` line. A hazard that hits never gives one. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 3.
- **Coin (debug mode):** every `drifter touch id=<n> type=coin effect=collect` line is followed by one `sound play cue=coin` line. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 3.
- **Overlap (debug mode, the test spawn list or any run with two cues on one tick):** two cues on one tick give two `sound play` lines, and no `sound dropped` line while the channels suffice. By hand: both sounds are heard together. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 9 and 10.
- **Silent while frozen (debug mode):** while paused, on a menu page and on the game-over screen, no `sound play` line appears, even right after a pause that came with a cue on its last stepped tick. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 11.
- **Levels (by hand):** the loop sounds quieter than the hit at the file's levels, and the graze is the quietest effect. Each follows its level in `audio.json`; changing a value there changes the loudness on the next page load. The hit at its level is heard clearly. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 12 and 13.
- **No mixer (desktop run on a machine or setting with no sound device):** where the mixer cannot start, one WARNING `audio off: ...` shows, the game runs as usual and no `sound` line appears. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 16.
- **Bad file:** a missing `audio.json`, a field out of range or a missing sound file shows the error screen, and the log names the file and the field. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 14 and 15.
