# App, integration checks, DRA-159
Part of: app
Read when: you run or extend the browser checks for the music mute (DRA-159).

## Rules

Integration checks for DRA-159, on top of the required browser checks and every earlier check, DRA-158's included (all must still pass). Each check starts in a fresh page unless it says otherwise. A browser test cannot hear: the checks read the `music pause` and `music resume` log lines of `app.audio` (DEBUG, so debug mode `?debug=1`) and the pixels of the icon's corner. The Director listens by hand for what the log can't show. The icon is a picture in the corner (`docs/contracts/systems/mute-icon.md`); its rectangle is the window's size minus `margin` and `size` from `tuning/mute_icon.json`. The icon checks compare screenshots of that rectangle between muted and unmuted.

Checks:
- **Mute and resume (debug mode):** from the menu press M once: one `music pause` line. Press M again: one `music resume` line. No `music start` line after the first, and none ever for a mute. Source: systems/audio.md, Rules 19 and Logging.
- **Same spot (real browser, by hand, the one open browser question):** let the loop play to a part that is easy to recognize, press M, wait about five seconds, press M. The music continues from the part where it stopped, not from the beginning of the loop and not from where it would be had it played on. If it restarts from the beginning or does not come back, stop and report: a contract change, not a fix. Source: systems/audio.md, Rule 24; 02-game-flow.md, rule 13.
- **Effects never mute (debug mode, by hand):** mute, then play: every hit, graze and coin still logs its `sound play cue=...` line and is heard at the same level as before. Source: systems/audio.md, Rule 20.
- **Every screen after the start page (debug mode):** M toggles on the menu, the instructions page, the leaderboard, in play, while paused, in the last life and on the game-over screen, with the name picker showing and not. Each press gives its `music pause` or `music resume` line. M never changes the menu selection, the picker, RETRY or MENU, never pauses or resumes the run and never moves the ship. Source: 02-game-flow.md, `mute_key`; systems/app/frame-loop.md, Music mute.
- **Not on the start page:** on the start page, before the first click or key, press M: no `music pause` line and no icon. Pressing M as the very first key opens the game with the music playing and unmuted: no `music pause` line, no icon. Source: systems/app/frame-loop.md, Music mute; 15-tech-constraints.md, rule 3.
- **Fresh press only (debug mode):** hold M for two seconds: exactly one `music pause` line, none from the hold. Hold M while the screen changes (for example during a pause): no second toggle. Source: 02-game-flow.md, `mute_key`.
- **The mute stays (debug mode):** mute, then pause and resume, lose focus, lose the last life, reach game over, RETRY, go back to the menu and start a new run. Never a `music resume` line and no sound of music until M is pressed again. Source: systems/audio.md, Rule 22.
- **Not saved:** mute, then reload the page and pass the start page: the music plays, no icon, no `music pause` line. The localStorage keys hold nothing new. Source: systems/audio.md, Rule 21.
- **The icon:** it shows in the bottom-right corner of the window on every screen after the start page while muted, and on none while the music plays. It never covers a pixel of the play area, adds no text and moves nothing of the HUD, the pause box or the game-over box: compare a screenshot of each screen before and after muting, and only the icon's rectangle differs. Source: 10-ui.md, `mute_icon_place`; systems/mute-icon.md, Rules 6 to 9.
- **Place and look (by hand, once):** the icon's rectangle sits `margin` px from the window's right and bottom edges and is `size` px square, both from `tuning/mute_icon.json`. It reads as a speaker with a slash, pale speaker, fuchsia slash, sharp (not blurred) at 1x. Source: 10-ui.md, `mute_icon_look`; systems/mute-icon.md, Rules 1, 2 and 6.
- **Dimmed with the screen (debug mode):** muted, on the pause screen and on the game-over screen, the icon is visible and darker than on PLAYING (compare the pixel colors of the same spot of the picture): the dim darkened it. The pause box and the game-over box do not cover it. On the menu, the instructions and the leaderboard it is not darkened: the same pixels as on PLAYING. Source: systems/mute-icon.md, Rule 11; systems/app/frame-loop.md, Rules, The mute icon.
- **Still:** with the music muted, screenshots of the icon's rectangle taken a few seconds apart on one screen are pixel for pixel equal. No blink, no pulse. Source: systems/mute-icon.md, Rule 5.
- **Unmute removes it:** press M again: the rectangle goes back to what it showed before the first mute, with the same HUD and screen pixels around it. Source: systems/mute-icon.md, Rules 8 and 10.
- **A bad file stops the page (desktop, by hand):** a `mute_icon.json` whose margin puts the icon over the play area, or a `size` that differs from the picture, shows the error screen and the log names `tuning/mute_icon.json` and the field. Restore the file afterward. Source: systems/mute-icon.md, Rules 14 to 16.
- **No mixer (desktop run with no sound device):** M does nothing, no icon, no `music pause` line. Source: systems/audio.md, Rule 23; systems/mute-icon.md, Rule 13.
