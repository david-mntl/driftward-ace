# App, integration checks, DRA-148
Part of: app
Read when: you run or extend the browser checks for the level-up moment (DRA-148).

## Rules

Integration checks for DRA-148, on top of the required browser checks and every earlier check (all must still pass). Each check starts in a fresh page unless it says otherwise. Background:
- `game/tuning/level_up.json` exists and holds the starting values the main session wrote, each key as `docs/contracts/schemas/level-up.schema.json` names it.
- A level rise needs a played level. The Integration Engineer reaches one with a scratch copy of `game/waves/` and `game/tuning/` as the earlier wave checks do (a short Level 1 playlist), never by editing the committed files. Ticks are the run's own; times are ticks / 60. Positions are on the 640 x 480 play area, whose window origin is (16, 10).
- Debug mode shows the banner the same as normal mode (the test spawn list has no waves, so use `play: waves`).

Checks:
- **Tuning loaded:** the log shows `level up loaded level_up_rest=<n> arrival_tick=<n> banner_ticks=<n>` with the file's values, and no error. Source: `systems/level-up/tuning.md`, Rules.
- **The rise:** on the tick the log shows `level up level=2`, the next line is `rest start ticks=<rest>` with the rest equal to the rest gap plus `level_up_rest` (08-difficulty.md, rule 10). No `wave start` follows until that many ticks have passed. Wave 0 ending logs no `rest start` (a Level 1 wave follows at once) and no banner shows. Source: `systems/level-up/core.md`, Rules.
- **The banner, frame by frame** (sampled with screenshots at ages 5, 15, 22, 26, 30, 45, 70, 110, 120, 130; the clock is the run's ticks, read from the log's `level up` line): at age 5 hairlines and glass are opening; at age 15 the number is falling and stretched above the middle; at age 22 it has landed, squashed wide, with a ring and streaks starting around it; at age 26 the whole play area picture is at its lowest, `bump.pixels` lower than at age 45 (compare the same star); at age 30 LEVEL is fading in above the number and pips appear below it; at age 45 a glint crosses the number; at age 70 the number, LEVEL and pips hold; at age 110 LEVEL and pips are gone and the number is small, over the play area, on its way to the HUD level box, and the band is closing; at age 120 the number is almost in the box; at age 130 the HUD level box shows the new level, larger than normal and settling, with its outline flashing. No fuchsia pixel in the play area at any age. Source: `systems/level-up/view.md`, Presentation.
- **The HUD keeps the old level until arrival:** the HUD level number and climb bars show the old level on every frame up to the arrival tick, and the new level from it. The debug overlay's `level` line (F3 on, debug mode) shows the new level from the rise tick. Source: `systems/level-up/core.md`, Rules (The shown level).
- **Behind the world:** with a drifter and the ship over the band (hold a ship on the middle row), both are drawn over the glass and the number and are not covered; only the number's flight, from `fly.start`, passes over them. Source: `systems/level-up/view.md`, Presentation.
- **Pause:** pausing in the middle of the banner freezes it, the flight and the bump in place; resuming goes on from the same age. Source: `systems/level-up/core.md`, Rules (Pause).
- **Last life during the banner:** losing the last life during the banner removes the banner and the pop at once, with no bump, and the HUD shows the run's real level; the game-over screen and the saved level are the real level. Source: `systems/level-up/core.md`, Rules (The banner, 1).
- **Retry and a new run** start with no banner and the HUD at Level 1.
- **Level 8:** after Level 8's playlist ends no banner shows and no rest is added. Source: 08-difficulty.md, rule 10.
- **Frame rate (the one that matters):** measure the frame time in the browser build, with the field as empty as the rest leaves it, at age 0 (nothing drawn), age 60 (the banner at full height) and age 110 (the flight), and report all three against a frame with no banner. The glass is the expected cost (`systems/level-up/view.md`, Presentation, Cost: estimate, not a measurement). Report it next to the Frame-rate check table in 12-art-and-audio.md; if the banner takes the frame over its budget, say so and name the tuning escape hatches (`band.alpha` 0, `band.height`, `bump.pixels` 0). Source: 12-art-and-audio.md, Frame-rate check; 15-tech-constraints.md, rule 5.
- **Startup:** report how long the page takes to start with the level-up images built, against a build without them. Source: `systems/level-up/view.md`, Presentation, Cost (startup).
- **Bad files:** one at a time in a scratch copy: `level_up_rest` 0 turns the feature off (no rest, no banner, no pop, no cue: no error); `banner_ticks` above `level_up_rest` (message names `tuning/level_up.json`, `banner_ticks`); a `colors.glass` of `nope` (names `colors.glass`); an unknown field; the file deleted (a `FileNotFoundError` naming its path). Each time the log shows ERROR `unexpected error, showing the error screen` with that error, and no `error screen failed` line. Source: `systems/level-up/tuning.md`, Rules.
- **Package contents:** `core/level_up.py`, `ui/level_up_view.py`, `ui/level_up_motion.py`, `ui/level_up_rules.py`, `app/level_up_tuning.py` and `tuning/level_up.json` must be in `game.apk`.
