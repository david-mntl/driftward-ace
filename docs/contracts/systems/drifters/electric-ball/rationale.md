# Drifters, the electric ball, rationale
Part of: drifters
Read when: you ask why the electric ball is built the way it is.

## Rationale

- **The charge and the fire show only with the ball (DRA-111).** Rule 4 says so. Rule 6 removes the electric ball once the ball is fully below the floor, and 07-drifters.md, rule 12 says nothing it draws may still be on screen then. The pulse circle reaches far past the ball, so an outline drawn while the ball is gone would vanish in view. Tying the charge look to the same test as the fire keeps rules 4, 6 and 12 true at once, and keeps the start "fully off screen, touching the top edge" on the ball alone, as rule 1 says. The other readings break a GDD line: judging the start and the leaving on the circle contradicts rule 6, and drawing the outline off screen contradicts rule 12. The player sees the outline arrive with the ball and go with it.
- **Its size is the ball, not the circle (DRA-111).** `width` and `height` are what the ball is, and the warning copy, the debug label and the start are about the ball. The circle lives in the hitbox. The loader skips the footprint check for this file, as for the UFO's dome: the circle lies outside the ball by design, and 07-drifters.md, rule 8 still holds because the firing look fills exactly that circle.
- **The pulse box joins `drawn_parts()` only while the ball is on screen (DRA-111).** `drawn_parts()` must hold every pixel drawn while ACTIVE. Off screen nothing but the ball is drawn, so the ball's box alone is right; on screen the circle and the rays are, so their box joins. Entering and leaving then come out exactly as on the ball, which is what rule 6 asks. `PULSE_DRAW_MARGIN` covers a circle drawn at a rounded center with a rounded radius, the same reason as the fireball's `DRAW_MARGIN`; it is not a game value.
- **The ball is on screen by its box (DRA-111).** "Any part visible" with the strict test of the base's `is_partly_on_screen`, the same as the chain lightning's node square. One meaning of "on screen" across the game.
- **Its own module, no shared class with the chain lightning (DRA-111).** The GDD calls it a chain lightning with one node, but the structure is one level deep with no middle classes (scope report DRA-29). The cycle is three short methods; repeating them costs less than coupling two drifters, and the registration guard keeps each drifter's type named only in its own modules.
- **Timings are type values, sizes are variant values (DRA-111).** wave_check asks a drifter for its telegraph through `telegraph_ticks(type_extra)`, which sees the type's values, not a variant's. With one variant that loses nothing. The GDD's How to extend makes another pulse timing a new variant; that needs the timings moved into the variant and the hook taught to read them, a design change for that feature (scope report DRA-111, Out: another pulse size or timing).
- **`invert` is accepted and does nothing (DRA-111).** Rule 8 says it has no effect, not that it is refused, so `born` overrides the base refusal. A placement with it plays exactly as one without.
- **The outline brightens in the ray steps (DRA-111).** The GDD's telegraph says the outline "brightens with" the rays. Using the ray count as its level makes them change on the same ticks, with integer math only, and full color always shows for the last part of the charge, at every level.
- **Widths and the fill's opacity are in the file; colors stay in the view (DRA-111).** "Everything configurable" covers sizes and thicknesses, as the chain lightning's `ray_width`, and the scope report keeps the fill alpha in tuning. Colors are placeholder look values; every view keeps them as constants with their GDD source, and art will replace them.
- **The fill is pre-rendered (DRA-111).** A translucent circle needs its own alpha surface; building it every frame would cost the browser build for nothing. The image depends only on its key, so a kept entry is never wrong.
- **A top-start ball fires once at the GDD's guesses (DRA-111).** Its speed and the screen height give it less time on screen than one charge and one fire take, so it fires once, from mid-screen, and leaves during that fire; it never charges a second time. The chain lightning does the same. That is what the guesses give; the contract doesn't change them. It is a tuning point for the Director, and the debug list has one slow row so the whole cycle can be seen.
- **No art task (DRA-111).** Final art and sound are out of scope (scope report DRA-111, Out). Every look is a placeholder drawn in code.
