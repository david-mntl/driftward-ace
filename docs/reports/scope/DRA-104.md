## Feature
A UFO is a saucer with a green dome that crosses the screen from one side to the other. By default it enters at the left wall and flies right. `invert` mirrors it: it enters at the right wall and flies left.

Its entry warning shows first, at its placed y against the wall it enters from. Then the UFO appears and flies across at a fixed speed. It never stops and never turns back. The scroll doesn't add to its speed.

It flies level at its placed y until it is fully on screen. After that it weaves. About every 1.3 s it picks a new height 40 to 120 px away, up and down in turns, and eases toward it. It never turns faster than a set rate, so where it heads next can be read from where it is going now. Its new height always stays inside a band on screen. If there is no room one way, it goes the other way.

Its path comes from a seed, never from the ship. The same wave flies the same path every time. A placement may set `seed` to pick another path. Two UFOs in one wave never share a path.

Touching the disc or the dome is a hit. It flies through walls and other drifters. It is removed once nothing of it is on screen past the far wall. After it takes a life, it dims for the re-hit cooldown, like every hazard.

In debug mode, the test spawn list can place UFOs and play only them.

## Questions
1. **Debug rows (not in GDD).** Proposal, same pattern as the satellite and planet rows: `type` ufo; `y` required and inside the band; optional `invert`, `seed` and `speed` (speed is the stand-in every row already has, level scaling still applies on top). A row with y outside the band is refused like any bad row. A UFO-only mode. Example rows: left entry, right entry with `invert`, and two UFOs at different heights with different seeds. Do you approve?
2. **Band in pixels.** The band (y 188 to 440) is a screen zone given in bare pixels. 03-world-and-camera.md, How to extend, says a new screen-space zone should be a ratio of `screen_h`. The spec also cites "the ship's zone (rule 2)", but rule 2 only defines the top line at y 168. The band does sit inside the area between the top line and the floor. My advice: build as written, in pixels, as a tuning value like everything else. If you want a ratio or a cleaner cite, that is your edit to the GDD, and I won't touch it. Which do you want?
3. **wave_check.** I put the UFO's band check and its hazard counting in scope, built by the main session after QA, as for the planet. The bot and gap checks stay out. OK?
