## Feature
The game keeps a top 10 of runs in the browser's localStorage. Each entry is a nickname, a score and the level reached. It also remembers the last nickname confirmed. Nothing else is saved.

There is no separate name-entry screen. After the 2-second last life, the game-over screen shows, and it holds everything. From its first frame it shows the message for the run's place: NEW BEST SCORE for #1, TOP 3 for #2–3, TOP 10 for #4–10, GAME OVER off the list. The score shows under it.

If the score makes the top 10, the letter picker shows on the same screen. It has 10 slots and starts filled with the last nickname, or blank if there is none. The cursor starts on the first slot. Up and down change the character in the current slot. Left and right move between slots. Both wrap around. Characters are A–Z, 0–9, space and `. - _ ! ? *`. Enter or Space saves. Blank slots at the end are dropped. A saved name adds the entry and becomes the last nickname. A name of only blanks saves nothing and keeps the last nickname as it was.

After the save, the picker stops taking keys and RETRY and MENU from DRA-125 take over. Up and down select, Enter or Space picks. A score off the list has no picker: RETRY and MENU work from the start, as in DRA-125. The message never changes after the screen opens.

The top 10 is ordered by score, high first. Same score: level reached, high first. Same score and level: the older entry stays above. A score that ties #10 on both is off the list.

BEST shows next to SCORE on the HUD. It is the #1 score on the list, and it changes only when an entry is saved. It is hidden while the list is empty or saves don't work.

The leaderboard page shows the top 10 in order: place, nickname, score, and the level reached next to the score.

If localStorage is empty, missing or broken, the game still runs. No BEST, no picker, nothing saved, and the game-over screen shows GAME OVER and the score. The leaderboard page opens empty.

Debug mode saves to the same top 10 as normal mode.

Every screen is a stand-in: plain text and shapes, until UI-01 and UI-04.

## Player sees
Finish a first run with a score: the game-over screen shows NEW BEST SCORE, the score and a blank picker with the cursor on the first slot. Pick a name and press Enter: RETRY and MENU become active. Press Enter: a new run starts with BEST next to SCORE. Reload the page and open Leaderboard: the run is listed with its place, name, score and level. Make the top 10 again: the picker starts filled with that name.
