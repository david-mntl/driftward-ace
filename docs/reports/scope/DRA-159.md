## Request
"A music mute mode during play: the background music stops, the effects keep playing." Director's answers in chat: the key is M, "just for music not for gameplay"; the mute is not saved, "on reset mute will reset"; music only, effects always on; no HUD or pause text, a small icon in the bottom-right corner.

## Feature
The M key mutes and unmutes the music. While muted, the music is silent and the effects keep playing as before.

M works on every screen after the start page, the pause and the game-over screen included, so the player can mute on the menu too. Muting stops the music where it is. Pressing M again resumes it from the same spot, never from the start. The mute stays on across runs and screens until the player presses M again or reloads the page.

A small icon in the bottom-right corner of the window shows that the music is muted, on every screen after the start page. No icon shows while the music plays. The icon is its own element: it adds no text to the HUD or the pause box and does not change the HUD layout.

The mute is not saved. A page reload starts with the music on.

## Limits
In: the M key, the muted icon.

Out:
- Saving the mute in any way. No save field, no schema change.
- Any other new key.
- A mode that turns all sound off, or turns effects off.
- Text about the mute on the HUD, the pause box or any screen.
- Volume sliders.

## Player sees
Press M during play: the music stops, the icon shows in the bottom-right corner, and a graze or coin still sounds. Press M again: the music is back and the icon is gone.
