## Feature
After pygbag's click-to-start, a menu shows Play, Instructions and Leaderboard, in that order, with Play selected. The selected item is marked. Up and down move the selection and wrap around: up on Play goes to Leaderboard, down on Leaderboard goes to Play. Enter or Space picks it.

Every Enter or Space press does one thing only. A press counts only when the key was up before it, on every screen. A key held from the screen before does nothing on the new one. So the press that picks Play never pauses the run, and the press that closes a page never picks a menu item.

Instructions opens one page with one section per topic: flying with the arrows (holding a key adds thrust; letting go, the ship keeps drifting while gravity pulls it down), pausing with Enter or Space, what a graze is, what a warning is, falling off the bottom costs a life, and coins. Each section has a stand-in icon. Leaderboard opens the top 10 page. It is empty until saves ship. Enter or Space on either page goes back to the menu.

Play starts a run, exactly as a run starts today. The game-over screen's MENU is the only way back to the menu during a session.

During a run, Enter or Space pauses. So does the browser tab or window losing focus. A PAUSE box shows in the middle of the screen. Everything freezes: physics, scroll, drifters, timers, invulnerability, cooldowns and the respawn wait. Enter or Space resumes. Getting focus back does not resume. Arrows do nothing while paused. The pause has no other options.

When the last life is lost, the ship bursts into a stand-in burst and never rebuilds. After a fall, the burst plays at the bottom edge, where the ship left the screen. For 2 seconds the world keeps scrolling and drifters keep moving while the particles float. Nothing can hit the ship. The score is frozen: no distance, coins or grazes count. Keys do nothing, and losing focus doesn't pause. The lost heart disappears as it does today; there is no screen flash and no HUD life break yet.

Then the game-over screen shows. Everything is frozen. It shows GAME OVER, the score, and two choices: RETRY, with MENU under it. RETRY is selected first. Up and down move the selection and wrap around, as on the menu. Enter or Space picks it. RETRY starts a new run straight away; everything resets. MENU goes back to the menu, with Play selected. Arrows no longer retry: they only move the selection.

Every screen is a stand-in: plain text and shapes. The real text, look, fonts and icons come with UI-03 and UI-04.

## Player sees
Click to start: the menu shows with Play selected. Press up: Leaderboard is selected. Open Instructions and Leaderboard, and go back with Enter. Press Play, then Space: a PAUSE box shows and everything stops. Switch tabs and come back: still paused. Press Space: the run goes on. Lose the last life: the ship bursts, the world moves on for 2 seconds, then GAME OVER, the score, RETRY and MENU show, RETRY selected. Press Enter: a new run starts. Lose again, press down, press Enter: the menu shows with Play selected.
