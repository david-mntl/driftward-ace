## Feature
Flying close to a hazard and getting away clean pays points. Every hazard that grazes has a graze zone: its hitbox grown by 14 px on every side. When the ship leaves a hazard's zone without having touched that hazard, the score goes up by 25. Each hazard pays this once, on the first clean exit.

The zone going away with the ship still inside also counts as a clean exit: the hazard leaves the screen, or a chain lightning or electric ball stops firing.

Only the ship's state at the moment it leaves counts. A ship that entered while invulnerable pays if its invulnerability has ended. A ship waiting to respawn never grazes. A hovering ship is still flying and can graze. No graze pays while the ship is invulnerable. A hazard that hits the ship never pays a graze. Coins, floating islands, walls and lava never pay one.

Grazes stack with no cap and no multiplier. The score becomes meters + coin points + 25 × grazes.

When a graze pays, three things happen at once. A white `+25` pops up at the ship's center and rises like the coin popup. A thin white ring grows out from the ship and fades. The HUD's graze count goes up by one. The count shows on HUD line 2, below SCORE, as `GRAZE 5`. It starts at 0 and goes back to 0 on retry.

## Player sees
Skim past a meteorite without touching it: a white `+25` rises from the ship, a white ring flashes out around it, SCORE jumps by 25 and GRAZE goes from 0 to 1. Touch one and lose a life: no popup, no ring, GRAZE stays put.
