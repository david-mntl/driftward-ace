## Request
"The level up moment: A new level should feel like a reward. A short screen animation announces the level, and the level readout in the HUD gets its own pop. The animation leaves a hook for the level-up jingle, which lands when we add sound. Would be amazing if the number of the new level appears comes from the top in the middle of the screen. Where levels change: A level ends when its wave playlist ends (08-difficulty, rule 1). The animation fires on that tick. Take into consideration that playlist could have extra time at the end, Either we add a special time which is gonna be fixed between levels so we can add this animation before the next wave of the new level starts or we use the time at the end of the last wave. I would prefer the first approach. It will happen before a new wave starts so it wont bother the player. I will be out, so you are in charge of selecting the motion and the banner and colors. It should feel amazing to receive this because you survived the wave, a small screen movement and a sound (which will be added when we add sounds later) and the animation should reflect that. It should be beautiful and the reward should feel worth it." (Director)

## Feature
When a level rises, the game adds a fixed level-up rest to the gap before the next wave. During that rest, a banner announces the new level.

- On the tick the level rises, the `level_up` sound cue fires. Nothing plays yet.
- A see-through glass band opens across the middle of the play area, with cyan hairlines at its top and bottom edges.
- The new level number drops in from above the play area, eases out with a small overshoot, and lands. On landing it squashes wide, a gold ring and short light streaks burst out, and the play area's picture bumps 3 px down and springs back.
- `LEVEL` fades in above the number. A row of 8 pips under it shows the levels reached, and the new pip flashes. One glint sweeps across the number. Then it holds.
- `LEVEL` and the pips fade. The number shrinks and flies on an arc into the HUD level box, and the band closes behind it.
- On arrival, the HUD level readout switches to the new level and pops: it starts large, settles with a small overshoot, and flashes white back to its tier color. The new climb bar and the level box outline flash.
- Until arrival, the HUD keeps showing the old level.

Timeline, colors, type and sizes: away log D2 and D3. The banner uses the HUD palette and the Glass Cockpit look. No fuchsia. No blur glow.

The level-up rest is `level_up_rest` ticks. It is added to the rest gap after the last wave of a level. The scroll never stops, so distance and score keep running. Drifters of the last wave still on screen keep flying.

Rules for edge cases:
- Wave 0 to Level 1 is not a rise: no banner.
- Level 8 is the cap: the level never rises again, so no banner after it.
- A skipped level (empty playlist) shows the level actually reached.
- Pause freezes the banner, the pop and the bump.
- A hit or a fall during the banner changes nothing about it.
- If the last life is lost during the banner, the banner and the pop stop at once and the HUD shows the run's real level.
- A new run starts with no banner.
- Debug mode shows it the same. The debug overlay's wave line reads `wave rest`.

## Player sees
Clear the last wave of a level: the play area bumps 3 px, a gold level number drops from the top into the center of the screen with a ring and streaks, and holds for a moment. Then it flies into the HUD level box, which pops to the new level. No wave starts until it is done.

## Objections
1. The band and the number sit on the play area. Source: Pillar 2; 10-ui.md rule 1 ("The HUD never covers the play area"). The away log keeps drifters visible by making the band see-through (D1). It does not cover the ship, which sits behind an opaque 88 px number if it is mid-screen, or entry warnings. Entry warnings of the next wave are not shown during the rest, but side-entry warnings of the last wave's late drifters can be. Alternative: keep the opaque number out of the ship's lane by dropping its alpha while the ship or a warning is behind it, or fix the number's alpha at about half until the landing. Open for the Director on return. Not a pillar break: the player can still see everything through the glass, and no wave starts.
2. The banner puts gold and warm streaks in the play area. Source: 12-art-and-audio.md, color language (orange and yellow mean heat, danger, entry warnings) and rule 14 (gold is a HUD-only meaning). A gold ring bursting in the play area could read as an entry warning or a fireball. Alternative: make the ring and streaks warm white or cyan, and keep gold on the number only (it matches the HUD box). Open for the Director on return.
3. The rest is up to 2.5 s of empty scroll per level rise. Distance and score keep running with no wave on. Source: Pillar 3 ("score for hiding"). Rest gaps already work this way (09-waves.md, rest gap), so this is not new. Over 7 rises it adds up to about 17.5 s of free distance. Alternative: score does not run during the level-up rest. That needs a scoring change the Director has not asked for. Open for the Director on return.
