## Feature
An electric ball is a single purple ball that falls straight down from the top. Its entry warning shows first, at its x against the top edge. Then it appears just above the top edge and falls into view at a fixed speed. The scroll doesn't add to its speed. It never aims at the ship.

The moment it appears, it starts to charge. Blue rays grow around the ball, more as the charge fills, and a faint outline of its pulse circle brightens with them. The outline shows exactly where the strike will land.

When the charge ends, the circle fires: it fills with lightning for 2 s. Then the next charge starts. Charge, fire, charge, fire, until it leaves. It fires only while some part of the ball is on screen. If the ball leaves while firing, the circle stops at once.

Only the firing circle hurts. Touching it is a hit. The ball itself and the charge outline never hit. After it takes a life, it dims for the re-hit cooldown, like every hazard. It is removed once the ball is fully below the floor.

In debug mode, the test spawn list can place electric balls and play only them.
