## Objections
1. Soft glow on screens contradicts the GDD. `10-ui.md` row `ui_glow` says "none. The HUD and screens stay flat". `12-art-and-audio.md` row `glow` and decision log row 97 say glow belongs to hazards, warnings and coins only. "Everywhere" could also mean the HUD, which is built flat. I won't resolve it. Alternative: glow on the five screens only; the HUD stays flat; rows `ui_glow` and `glow` and decision 97 are changed by the Director to say so.
2. The palette changes the HUD's colors. The pasted keys reuse HUD keys with other values (`ship_lines` #ff00ae to #9d6bff, `level` #ffc800 to #ffd166, `plating`, `score`, `panel_body`, `frame_lights`, `last_life`, `hearts`, `graze`). The HUD palette is set by decision 113 and lives in `hud.json`. If screens reuse the keys, the HUD turns violet. Alternative: add the Mono Violet set as its own screens palette in the tuning file, with the three `rank_*` keys, and leave the HUD palette as it is. Or the Director changes the HUD palette too, with a new decision-log row.
3. Mono type on screens contradicts the font row. Glass Cockpit sets titles, digits, buttons, rows and captions in Share Tech Mono. `10-ui.md` rows `ui_font` and `hud_fonts` and decision 114 say Exo 2 Bold for every screen, with Share Tech Mono for small HUD labels only. Alternative: titles and values in Exo 2 Bold, mono only for small labels and key hints. Or the Director widens the mono rule to the screens.
4. The pause hint adds content to a locked row. `pause_box` says "a box in the middle of the screen with the word PAUSE". The brainstorm flagged the hint as an extra idea; "Key hints on" reads as the Director choosing it. Alternative: accept it as chosen, and the Director edits `pause_box` to add it. The picker hints and the page footer are a separate yes or no (Question 5).
5. The Glass Cockpit page frame adds invented text and marks. The full-window pages carry corner labels (`DRIFTWARD · HOW TO PLAY`, `SYS OK ▮▮▮▯`, `4/05`, `HUD LINK ◉`) and side rulers. None is in the GDD, and `ui_voice` is terse. Corner brackets sit 1 px outside the box edge. Alternative: keep the brackets and rulers as pure shapes, drop all corner text.
6. "Remove the pause icon" has no counterpart in the mock. The pause row shows a small pause glyph beside the word in every look. Read as: drop that glyph. Alternative: none needed, confirming the reading is enough.
