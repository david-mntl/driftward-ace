## Feature
After the start-page key press, the lo-fi guitar loop starts. It plays on the menu and carries on into the run.

Three effects play on game events:
- Hit: every time the ship loses a life, by touching a drifter or by falling off the floor. The last life included, until a game-over sound exists.
- Graze: when a graze pays.
- Coin: when a coin is collected.

Effects can overlap. Each sound's loudness is its own level times its master. All levels are config values.

The music plays without a break through the menu, a run, the pause and the game-over screen.

No sound plays before the player's first click or key press on the start page.
