## Decisions
- **M handling is in `_read_events`, on `KEYDOWN` only, with no screen check and no `set_repeat`.** `_toggle_mute` flips on `audio.music_paused`; the app keeps no copy of the state. Source: `systems/app/frame-loop.md`, Rules, Music mute.
- **`audio is not None` guards the key and the draw argument.** With no mixer M does nothing and `music_muted` is False, so no icon. Source: `systems/audio.md`, Rule 23; `mute-icon.md`, Rule 13.
- **`pause_music` is a no-op before `start_music`, and `start_music` sets `_music_paused` False.** `pause_music` calls `pygame.mixer.music.pause()` and `resume_music` calls `unpause()`: a pause, never a volume of 0, so it resumes where it stopped (proved in the browser). Each call that changes the state logs once at DEBUG. Source: `systems/audio.md`, Interface, Rules 19 and 24, Logging.
- **The icon is drawn in two places in `_draw`:** on the run screens right after `level_up_view.draw_flight` and before the pause branch (so before `hud.dim`, and under the boxes), and as the last call on the menu, instructions and leaderboard. Source: `systems/app/frame-loop.md`, Rules, The mute icon; `mute-icon.md`, Rule 11.
- **`_draw` gets two trailing arguments with defaults (`mute_icon_view=None`, `music_muted=False`),** so a caller that passes neither draws as before. Source: `systems/app/frame-loop.md`, Imports.
- **Load order:** `load_mute_icon_look(hud_rules)` right after `load_audio` (after `load_audio_levels`), the picture and view once after the display is open, all inside the guard. The loader logs one INFO line. Source: `systems/app/frame-loop.md`, Rules, The mute icon; `mute-icon.md`, Rule 17.
- **The tuning loader reuses `app.drifters.checks` (`check_fields`, `check_integer`)** like `audio_tuning`, so a bool is never an int. The two colors are checked as `#rrggbb` and dropped. Source: `mute-icon.md`, Rules 14 and 15; `game/CONTEXT.md`, Error handling.
- **Browser inputs as DOM events with a held gap and canvas pixel reads** instead of screenshots, as the role rules ask for numbers rather than image comparisons.
